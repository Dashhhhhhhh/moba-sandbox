Move: arrow keys or right-click. Aim with the mouse. Abilities: Q Stretch Grab (cast to grab an enemy, cast again to slam it), W Ooze Burst, E Elastic Launch (hold E to charge, release to fly), R Boing Barrage. F is Reap, the camp execute. Your passive, Loose Gel, drops healing chunks when you take damage, and on lethal damage you split into bloblets: keep at least one alive for 6 seconds to revive. Your champion auto-attacks the nearest enemy in range. Clear jungle camps, last-hit red minions, and level up. Push with your wave to take down the red towers; enemy champions and epic bosses are on the work board. On touch screens, drag the joystick to move and tap an ability, then the map, to aim.
